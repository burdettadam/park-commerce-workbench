## Expiry/confirmation race and rollback

`CheckoutRecoveryIT#expiryAndConfirmationAtBoundaryHaveOneReconciledOutcome` parks a real HTTP checkout at its transactional clock read while the event transaction is active. The test advances the controlled clock to the exact hold expiry and releases the checkout: HTTP returns `EXPIRED`, recovery persists the expired state, SQL has zero orders and three available seats. It then confirms a fresh hold before expiry, advances to its expiry and replays the original key: one persisted order survives with two available seats. These are both sides of the boundary under a controlled interleaving, not a throughput measurement.
