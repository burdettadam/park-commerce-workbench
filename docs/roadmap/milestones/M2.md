# M2 — Complete catalog and pricing administration

**Status: proposed milestone, not achieved.** Owner/acceptance authority is to be recorded when adopted. No deadline, release tag, human approval or cloud authorization is asserted.

## Observable milestone outcome

An employee can draft, preview, publish, and explain versioned prices without rewriting existing quotes.

## Milestone artifacts

Publication audit; preview scenarios; deterministic pricing fixture pack.

Every goal also retains its fixed-candidate manifest, independent expected results, raw checks, two independent reviewer reports, validation/decision record and recoverable handoff. Goal-specific artifact filenames are defined in its proposal; they are **required future outputs**, not files claimed to exist now.

## Exit gate

All three goals must have a current `ready` candidate under the adopted rules, with every acceptance criterion and mandatory artifact accounted for. The integration owner identifies exactly which source revisions enter the combined milestone candidate and reruns affected end-to-end checks on that combination. Passing each branch separately is insufficient.

Retain one milestone acceptance record using [the template](../templates/milestone-acceptance.md). The human records intended-use acceptance and unresolved operational limitations. No missing licensed tool, skipped database suite, stale review or unavailable artifact is silently waived. A material change requires an accepted revision and renewed affected evidence.

**Checkpoint meaning:** Pricing workbench demonstration.

`ready`, human-approved, merged, packaged, deployed, healthy and remotely recoverable are separate observations. Version-producing merges retain the human gate. No GitHub visibility change or external spending is authorized by milestone adoption.

## Workflow behavior to observe

Record plan revision, integration owner, branch writers and included revisions. Observe scope changes, stale evidence, independent first reviews, review rounds and recovery coverage in the [process experiment](../PROCESS-EXPERIMENT.md). Do not retroactively manufacture these records at the end.
